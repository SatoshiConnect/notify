Terms of Use

Last updated: November 22, 2026

NSAT Messenger is an independently operated prototype for testing realtime room chat, shared photos and voice clips, polls, and one-to-one calls.

Use of the service

Use an account you control, respect other participants, and do not use the service to break the law, harass people, or share content you do not have the right to share. You are responsible for the messages and call content you send.

Prototype service

The service is provided for evaluation and may be changed or unavailable. It has no end-to-end encryption, user-facing message deletion, group video calls, or production-grade account recovery. Do not rely on it for emergencies, confidential communication, or permanent storage.

Wallet keys

New members create a non-custodial wallet key on their device and are responsible for saving the recovery key somewhere safe. The wallet password protects the encrypted key stored in that browser. Anyone with the recovery key can control the wallet. The server does not receive or recover the key. Wallet balance and transfer features are not provided by this prototype.

Calls and permissions

Video and audio calls require browser permission to use your camera and microphone. Optional visual filters process your camera frames in your browser before sending them to the other caller. Screen sharing sends the screen you select to the other caller after browser permission. You can end a call or disable your camera and microphone using the in-app controls. Voice recording starts only when you choose the Voice control and requires microphone access.
